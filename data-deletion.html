Flash2Digest - User Data Deletion

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Flash2Digest is a privately operated news content and publishing tool.

Requesting Deletion of Your Data

If you have authorized Flash2Digest through Facebook and would like your data associated with Flash2Digest to be deleted, please send a deletion request by email to:

flash2digest@gmail.com

Please use the subject "Flash2Digest Data Deletion Request".

After receiving the request, data associated with your use of Flash2Digest will be reviewed and deleted where applicable.

Facebook Authorization

You may also remove Flash2Digest's access to your Facebook account through your Facebook settings. Removing the app will revoke its authorization to access Facebook data through the Meta Graph API.

Contact

For questions regarding data deletion, contact: flash2digest@gmail.com
