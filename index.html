Flash2Digest Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Flash2Digest is a privately operated news content and publishing tool. This Privacy Policy explains how Flash2Digest uses information when interacting with Meta and Facebook services.

Information We Access

Flash2Digest may access basic Facebook account information and information about Facebook Pages managed by the authorized user, as permitted through the Meta Graph API.

Depending on the permissions granted, this may include basic profile information, the list of Pages managed by the authorized user, and information related to Page posts and engagement.

How We Use Information

The information is used only to operate Flash2Digest, including identifying the authorized Facebook Page, publishing news content to that Page, and checking information about published Page posts for operational verification and diagnostics.

Flash2Digest does not sell Facebook user data and does not use Facebook data for advertising or profiling.

Data Sharing

Flash2Digest does not sell or provide Facebook user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or data-broker purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Facebook data is retained only when necessary for the operation and diagnostics of Flash2Digest. Users may request deletion of Facebook-related data by contacting Flash2Digest at the email address below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: flash2digest@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Flash2Digest functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
